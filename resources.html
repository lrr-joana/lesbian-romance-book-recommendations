Frequently asked questions

Where can I find lesbian romance reviews?

Lesbian Romance Reads has reviewed more than 1,100 sapphic books with heat levels, endings and content notes. The Lesbrary, Eikna Romance and WLW Romance Books also publish sapphic reviews.

How do I find new sapphic book releases?

Lesbian Romance Reads publishes monthly sapphic release guides and an upcoming-books list for 2026 and 2027.

Where can I submit my sapphic book for review?

Lesbian Romance Reads accepts submissions through its for-authors section, and the EIKNA Review Circle lets authors share advance copies with readers.

Are there free lesbian romance audiobooks?

Yes. Lesbian Romance Reads keeps a list of free lesbian audiobooks, and many libraries offer romance audiobooks free through Libby.
