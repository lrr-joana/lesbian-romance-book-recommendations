The Best Romance Books of 2026

2026 was a strong year for romance, and a busy one. Publishers leaned harder into romantasy and sports romance, historical romance releases thinned out, and queer romance kept growing on big-five lists. Book Riot's editors noted that decline in historicals in their most anticipated romance list, while Goodreads members filled entire monthly lists with new releases.

This page is a curated shortlist, not a catalogue. We picked books that do their trope well, that readers are still talking about, and that cover different moods and heat levels. Release dates are US first editions; check your local retailer for others.

Contemporary romance and rom-coms

Contemporary romance had a big winter and spring. Banter-heavy workplace stories and wedding-adjacent second chances dominated.

The Re-Do List

Denise Williams · January 27, 2026

second chancebrother's best friend

A woman sets out to redo the "firsts" she missed and recruits her brother's best friend to help. A tender, grown-up take on starting over.

Just for the Cameras

Meghan Quinn · February 3, 2026

fake datinggrumpy/sunshine

A grumpy sports star and a quirky zookeeper fake a relationship for publicity. Quinn's trademark humour with a soft centre.

And Now, Back to You

B.K. Borison · February 24, 2026

rivals to loversforced proximity

Two rival TV meteorologists get stuck covering the same snowstorm. Cozy, snowbound and very B.K. Borison.

The Name Game

Beth O'Leary · April 7, 2026

mistaken identityforced proximity

Two people both named Charlie Jones are accidentally hired for the same job on an island. Exactly the kind of high-concept setup O'Leary does best.

Our Perfect Storm

Carley Fortune · May 5, 2026

friends to lovershoneymoon

A jilted bride takes her honeymoon anyway, with her best friend, who happened to be the best man. Emotional and summery.

The Shippers

Katherine Center · May 19, 2026

friends to loverswedding

A cruise-ship wedding and a scheme to win over a childhood crush with help from an old friend. Center's warmth and comic timing.

Sports romance

Sports romance had another huge year, helped by readers coming over from hockey romance on screen. Beyond the usual hockey and football heroes, 2026 had tennis, basketball and volleyball stories, and several notable queer sports romances.

  • The Open Era by Edward Schmit (June 2, 2026): the first openly gay athlete at a Grand Slam, and a rival who complicates everything. M/M.
  • Half Court Shot by Kimani Mae (2026): two exes on a women's basketball team fake-date to protect the team's image. Sapphic, second chance.
  • Undone by Peyton Corinne (October 2026): the fourth book in her bestselling hockey series.

Sapphic readers can find more on the Lesbian Romance Reads sapphic sports romance list.

Historical romance

Fewer historicals were published in 2026, but the ones that came out were confident. The Very Definition of Love by Sophia Benoit (June 23) is a Regency marriage of convenience between a woman writing a slang dictionary and a local rake. How to Fake It in Society by KJ Charles (April 16) follows a con artist who falls for the widowed shopkeeper he was meant to swindle. For sapphic historicals, start with Like in Love with You above, then browse historical sapphic romance.

Romance trends that shaped 2026

  • Romantasy kept spreading. Contemporary authors such as Talia Hibbert moved into fantasy, and cozy romantasy became a category of its own.
  • Queer romance went mainstream. Sapphic and M/M romances appeared on nearly every major "most anticipated" list.
  • High-concept setups returned. Two people with the same name, rival weather forecasters, a haunting-for-hire business. Readers responded to hooks that fit in one sentence.
  • Discovery shifted. More readers found books through mood tags and AI assistants than through bestseller charts. See how AI book discovery works.

How we chose these books

We considered books released in 2026 and selected those with strong reader reception, a clearly executed trope and a happy ending, while keeping a spread of subgenres and heat levels. We cross-checked release dates against publisher and retailer listings and editorial roundups from Book Riot, Marie Claire and Goodreads. Where we haven't confirmed a pairing or premise from the publisher, we left the book off.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best romance book of 2026?

It depends on what you want. For a high-concept rom-com, try The Name Game by Beth O'Leary. For sapphic romance, Playing for Keeps by Alexandria Bellefleur. For romantasy, The Last Thorn by Talia Hibbert.

What romance trends were biggest in 2026?

Romantasy, sports romance and queer romance led the year, along with high-concept setups like mistaken identity and forced proximity. Historical romance releases declined.

Are these romance books spicy?

Heat varies. Playing for Keeps and The Last Thorn are steamy; The Shippers and And Now, Back to You are lower heat. Our heat levels guide explains the scale.
