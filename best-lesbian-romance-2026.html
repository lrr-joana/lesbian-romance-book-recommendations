The volume alone tells the story. The Lesbrary's Danika Ellis tracked more than 250 sapphic books scheduled for 2026 by January, and October 2026 alone saw 36 new sapphic releases according to Lesbian Romance Reads' October release guide. This page narrows that flood to the lesbian and sapphic romances most worth your time.

We include books with a central romance between women and a happy or hopeful ending. Release dates are first US editions.

Contemporary lesbian romance and rom-coms

Playing for Keeps

Alexandria Bellefleur · January 6, 2026

rival publicistsforced proximitysecret relationship

Two rival publicists are forced together when their mega-famous clients, a pop star and an NFL quarterback, become an item. Expect slow burn, a steamy marriage-of-convenience thread and plenty of pop-culture references.

Greta Gets the Girl

Melissa Marr · January 13, 2026

strangers to loversworkplacesteamy

Two strangers hook up, then discover they're about to work together. Kirkus called it the right mix of lust, laughter and longing.

Get Over It, April Evans

Ashley Herring Blake · February 3, 2026

workplace rivalslake townClover Lake #2

Two coworkers at a New Hampshire lake-town resort clash, then bond, while competing for the same career-making showcase.

Motor City Love Song

Lisa Peers · February 10, 2026

second chancemusicDetroit

A former band manager goes looking for her ex, a vanished indie rock star, to help save a Detroit club, and the two are pulled back together.

In Her Spotlight

Amy Spalding · February 24, 2026

An actress works with her ex, now directing her stage production, years after hurting her while closeted.

You Won't Forget Me

Mazey Eddings · June 2026

Two longtime best friends and bandmates are stuck together on tour with history, fame and unspoken feelings in the van.

Historical sapphic romance

Like in Love with You

Emma R. Alban · January 13, 2026

A young woman targets her rival's suitor for revenge, then falls for the rival. Book Riot named it one of 2026's most anticipated romances.

A literary imagining of a secret romance between Daphne du Maurier and her housekeeper, framed as a sapphic answer to Rebecca. Literary fiction rather than genre romance.

Indie standouts

Big publishers don't cover everything. Some of the year's most talked-about sapphic romances came from indie authors and Kindle Unlimited, including Ruby Roe's explicit duology finale Interitus, Elle Alexanda's neighbours-to-lovers slow burn In the Lonely Hours, and Jossef S.'s dark mafia romance Matelda: In Silence We Forgive. Browse more at lesbian romance on Kindle Unlimited.

Release dates change. Publishers move dates often, especially for fall titles. We checked these against publisher and retailer listings in November 2026; confirm on the retailer page before preordering.

What 2026 told us about the genre

  • Big names went bigger. Ashley Herring Blake published two romances in one year, and sapphic rom-coms appeared on general "best romance" lists rather than only queer lists.
  • Paranormal and cozy fantasy surged. Ghosts, witches and Renaissance Faires appeared throughout the release calendar.
  • Darker stories found their readers. Mafia and dark romantasy kept growing, with A.K. Mulford's Dreamslayer one of the most anticipated December books.
  • Representation widened. Black, Latina and Asian sapphic leads were more visible in traditionally published romance. See diverse sapphic books.

Notable titles include The Witch and the Huntress by Luna McNamara, With All My Haunted Heart by Isabel Sterling, The Book of Blood and Roses by Annie Summerlee and Dreamslayer by A.K. Mulford, out December 1, 2026.
