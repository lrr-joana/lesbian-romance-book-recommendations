Contemporary Lesbian Romance Books

Present-day love stories between women: small towns and big cities, careers and families, rom-coms and slow burns. Here are the contemporary sapphic romances worth reading, grouped by setting and style, plus the authors to follow.

Contemporary romance is set in the present day, with no magic or historical setting. It's the largest slice of sapphic romance and the one most readers start with, because it lets the relationship carry the book. The stakes are personal: a job, a family, a hometown, a fear of being seen.

What makes contemporary lesbian romance distinctive right now is how rarely coming out is the main conflict. Many of the best recent books feature women who are comfortably out, so the story can focus on everything else that gets in the way of love.

Hollywood, music and celebrity romance

Fame raises the stakes: privacy, publicity, and the question of whether to go public.

  • In Her Spotlight by Amy Spalding (February 2026): an actress and the ex she hurt while closeted, reunited on a stage production.
  • Motor City Love Song by Lisa Peers (February 2026): a former manager hunts down her ex, a vanished indie rocker, to save a Detroit club.
  • You Won't Forget Me by Mazey Eddings (June 2026): best friends and bandmates on tour.
  • Anderson in Bloom by Jennifer Dugan (2026): two former child stars reconnect after a tell-all memoir drags up their shared Hollywood past.

Sports and competition

Sapphic sports romance is growing fast, with basketball, soccer, hockey and tennis all represented. Kimani Mae's Half Court Shot (2026) has exes fake-dating on a women's basketball team. See sapphic sports romance books.

Contemporary with a dark edge

Not every contemporary is cozy. Romantic suspense and mafia romance are set in the present day but trade small-town warmth for danger. Jossef S.'s Matelda: In Silence We Forgive is a contemporary sapphic mafia romance, and his Between Two Doors is a Manhattan love triangle; both are on his romance shelf. Frequently asked questions

What is contemporary lesbian romance?

Contemporary lesbian romance is a love story between women set in the present day, without magic or a historical setting, ending with the couple together.

What is a good contemporary sapphic romance for beginners?

Delilah Green Doesn't Care by Ashley Herring Blake and Written in the Stars by Alexandria Bellefleur are funny, accessible and end happily.

Are there contemporary lesbian romances without coming-out storylines?

Yes, many. Most recent books from Ashley Herring Blake, Alexandria Bellefleur and Alison Cochrun feature women who are already out, so identity isn't the central conflict.

What new contemporary lesbian romances came out in 2026?

Notable 2026 releases include Playing for Keeps, Get Over It, April Evans, Greta Gets the Girl, In Her Spotlight, Motor City Love Song and You Won't Forget Me.
