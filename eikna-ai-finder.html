EIKNA AI Book Finder: A Practical Guide

EIKNA is an independent book discovery platform that combines a searchable catalogue with an AI assistant called Lyra. Here's what it does, how to get great recommendations from it, and how romance and sapphic readers can make the most of it.

EIKNA describes itself simply: find the book you're in the mood for. It covers romance, fantasy, science fiction, mystery, thriller, horror, literary fiction, historical fiction, young adult and children's books, with romance as its deepest shelf. Three labels sit under its search bar: independent book discovery, evidence-backed book details, and AI-assisted.

That last part is the interesting one. Rather than asking a general chatbot to recall books from memory, EIKNA lets you search its own catalogue directly, or ask its assistant, Lyra, to help you choose from that catalogue. For why that distinction matters, see our guide to AI book recommendations.

Three ways to find a book on EIKNA

1. Search

The search bar on the homepage takes a title, author or keyword. Use it when you already have something in mind: an author you love, a series name, a word like "witch" or "hockey".

2. Browse the shelves

EIKNA organises books by genre. Start at all genres or jump straight to a shelf:

  • Romance: love stories for every mood, from slow burn to swoon, sweet to spicy.
  • Sapphic romance: love stories between women.
  • Fantasy, mystery, thriller, horror and more.
  • Tropes: browse by the story pattern you want.

The homepage also shows recently added books and a weekly featured selection.

3. Ask Lyra

Lyra is EIKNA's AI reading assistant. Instead of filtering by hand, you describe what you want in plain language and Lyra suggests books from the catalogue. It's the fastest route when your request is specific or hard to express as a single genre.

How to ask Lyra for better recommendations

Lyra handles natural language, so be as specific as you like. These are the kinds of requests that work well:

  • "A sapphic slow burn with an ice queen boss, open door, happy ending."
  • "Something cozy and low-stakes for a rainy weekend, with found family."
  • "Enemies to lovers fantasy with two women, not too dark."
  • "Books like Delilah Green Doesn't Care but set in a city."
  • "A romance where both heroines are over 40."
  • "A dark mafia romance, sapphic, with a morally grey heroine."

Include the details that matter to you: trope, heat level, mood, ending and any content you'd rather avoid. If the first answer is close but not right, refine it: "less angst", "more banter", "standalone only".

Reading lists and your account

EIKNA has reading lists and a personal my reading area, so you can save what Lyra suggests rather than losing it in a chat. You'll also find reading guides and author pages for going deeper.

The EIKNA Review Circle

EIKNA is soft-launching a program called the EIKNA Review Circle, which connects readers with free advance copies of new books shared by their authors.

  • For readers: request advance copies of books before publication and read them free in your browser. If you enjoy one, an honest review helps the author, but it's always optional. Start reading free.
  • For authors: share advance copies securely with readers who choose your book. You approve every reader; each copy is watermarked, read in the browser only, and never downloadable. It's free during the soft launch. Share your book.

EIKNA itself notes that the program is small and brand new, so expect the selection to grow over time.

EIKNA for romance readers

Romance is EIKNA's deepest shelf, and it pairs naturally with two companion sites from the same editorial team:

  • Eikna Romance: romance across every pairing, with researched assessments and guides to subgenres, tropes, heat and endings, plus how its ratings work.
  • Lesbian Romance Reads: 1,100+ sapphic book reviews, each with heat level, ending, content notes and a "Romance DNA" profile of trope, pace and POV.

A practical workflow: ask Lyra for three or four candidates, then open each one's full review on Eikna Romance or Lesbian Romance Reads to check heat and content notes before you commit.

Who EIKNA is for

  • Mood readers who know the feeling they want but not the title.
  • Trope readers who want a specific combination.
  • Sapphic readers tired of digging through general lists for books with women at the centre.
  • Authors looking for early readers through the Review Circle.

Independent authors are part of that catalogue too. Jossef S., who writes sapphic romance including Matelda: In Silence We Forgive, keeps his romance shelf at jossefsalman.com.

Frequently asked questions

What is EIKNA?

EIKNA is an independent, AI-assisted book discovery platform. You can search its catalogue, browse genre and trope shelves, or ask its assistant Lyra for recommendations.

What is Lyra on EIKNA?

Lyra is EIKNA's AI reading assistant. You describe what you want to read in plain language and Lyra suggests books from EIKNA's catalogue.

Is EIKNA free?

Searching, browsing and the Review Circle for readers are free to use. The Review Circle is also free for authors during its soft launch.

Does EIKNA have lesbian romance books?

Yes. Romance is EIKNA's deepest shelf, and it has a dedicated sapphic romance shelf for love stories between women.

What is the EIKNA Review Circle?

A new program where authors share advance copies of upcoming books and readers can read them free in the browser. Copies are watermarked and can't be downloaded, and reviews are optional.
