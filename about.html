About the Romance Books Discovery Hub

A free, independent guide to romance books, with a dedicated lesbian and sapphic romance section and an honest look at how AI is changing book discovery. Here's who we are, how we choose books, and how we keep recommendations accurate.

The Romance Books Discovery Hub exists for one reason: to help readers find a romance novel that fits them, faster. We organise books by the details romance readers actually choose by (trope, heat, mood, ending and representation) and link every recommendation to a full review or a deeper guide, so you can check before you commit.

Who curates this site

The site is curated by the Eikna Editorial Team, the editorial group behind:

  • EIKNA, an independent, AI-assisted book discovery platform.
  • Eikna Romance, romance reviews and guides across every pairing.
  • Lesbian Romance Reads, more than 1,100 sapphic book reviews.

We also work with independent author Jossef Salman, who writes sapphic romance under the pen name Jossef S., and with our sister blogs WLW Romance Books and Lesbian Romance Books.

What we cover

  • Romance books: the year's best releases, heat levels, books by mood and reading lists. Start at the best romance books of 2026.
  • Lesbian and sapphic romance: our largest section, with guides by subgenre and trope. Start at the lesbian romance books guide.
  • AI and modern book discovery: how readers find books in 2026, what AI does well and badly, and how to use discovery tools wisely. Start at the AI book discovery guide.

How we choose books

  1. The trope must be executed well. A book on our enemies-to-lovers page must actually be enemies to lovers, not two characters who argue once.
  2. Romance pages feature romance. Books on romance pages have a central love story and a happy or hopeful ending. When we include fiction that doesn't guarantee one, such as The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo, we say so.
  3. Range matters. We mix traditionally published and indie books, different heat levels, and different identities and settings.
  4. We prefer books with full reviews. Where a book has been reviewed in depth on Lesbian Romance Reads or Eikna Romance, we link to that review so you can check heat, ending and content notes.

How we check facts

Every release date, premise and series detail is checked against publisher or retailer listings and reputable editorial coverage such as Book Riot, Kirkus Reviews, Goodreads, The Lesbrary and Smart Bitches, Trashy Books. When we can't confirm a detail (say, whether an announced romance is F/F or M/F), we leave the book out rather than guess. Release dates can move after publication of a page; we note that on time-sensitive lists.

We don't reproduce book text or cover art. Descriptions are written in our own words.

How we use AI

We write about AI book discovery, and we use AI tools in our own research, mainly to brainstorm candidates and to organise notes. We never publish a book recommendation based on AI output alone; every title is confirmed to exist and checked against a real listing before it appears here. That's the same principle we recommend to readers in our AI book discovery guide.

Our relationship with linked sites

We link heavily to our own network (Lesbian Romance Reads, Eikna Romance, EIKNA, jossefsalman.com and our sister blogs) because that's where our full reviews and guides live. We also link to independent sources like The Lesbrary, Book Riot and Goodreads when they're the best reference.

Books by Jossef S., including Matelda: In Silence We Forgive, appear on some pages. Because the author is part of our network, we disclose that relationship here. We link to the book's full review on Lesbian Romance Reads, which is also part of our network, so you can check heat level and content notes for yourself.

Contact and corrections

Spotted a wrong date, a mislabelled trope or a book that belongs on one of our lists? We correct errors quickly. You can reach the editorial team through EIKNA or Lesbian Romance Reads. Authors who want their sapphic book considered for review can use Lesbian Romance Reads' submission page or the EIKNA Review Circle.
