Each list below is built around one reader need, ordered so you can read top to bottom, and linked to full reviews or bigger guides when you want more.

The romance starter pack

For readers new to the genre, or coming back after years away. These five books show the range of modern romance: rom-com, emotional contemporary, STEM, historical and fantasy.

  1. Book Lovers by Emily Henry: the definitive modern rivals-to-lovers rom-com. Review.
  2. The Kiss Quotient by Helen Hoang: tender, open door, with an autistic heroine. Review.
  3. The Love Hypothesis by Ali Hazelwood: fake dating in academia, the book that launched a thousand STEM romances. Review.
  4. Two Rogues Make a Right by Cat Sebastian: a quiet, loving M/M Regency. Review.
  5. Bride by Ali Hazelwood: a vampire and a werewolf in a marriage of convenience; a gateway to paranormal romance. Review.

Kindle Unlimited romance picks

Kindle Unlimited (KU) is Amazon's ebook subscription, and a huge share of indie romance is enrolled. KU is especially strong for trope-specific sapphic romance: ice queens, age gap, mafia and workplace stories that big publishers rarely buy.

Titles move in and out of KU, so check the "Read for Free" badge before borrowing. These guides are checked book by book:

Romance audiobooks

Narration can make or break a romance. Dual-narrator recordings work especially well for dual-POV love stories, and a good narrator can carry a slow burn through a long commute. Sapphic listeners should start with lesbian romance audiobooks and the guide to sapphic audiobook narrators. Many libraries offer romance audiobooks free through Libby.

Romance for book clubs

Book clubs need romances with enough to discuss beyond the couple: family, career, grief, identity. These work well:

  • Here We Go Again by Alison Cochrun: friendship, grief and a road trip.
  • Book Lovers by Emily Henry: sisterhood and ambition.
  • The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo by Taylor Jenkins Reid: not genre romance (no guaranteed HEA), but a sapphic love story told through a Hollywood life and very discussable.
  • The Lady's Guide to Celestial Mechanics by Olivia Waite: a sapphic Regency about women in science.
  • Matelda: In Silence We Forgive by Jossef S.: for darker book clubs, a sapphic mafia story about secrets and forgiveness. The reading guide and character guide make discussion easier.

Frequently asked questions

Where should I start with romance novels?

Start with a few widely loved books from different subgenres, such as Book Lovers, The Kiss Quotient and Delilah Green Doesn't Care, then follow the tropes you enjoyed.

What is the best sapphic romance for beginners?

Delilah Green Doesn't Care by Ashley Herring Blake and Written in the Stars by Alexandria Bellefleur are both funny, moderate-heat books with happy endings.

Are there lesbian romance books on Kindle Unlimited?

Yes, a large share of indie sapphic romance is on Kindle Unlimited. Check the Read for Free badge on each book because enrolment changes.

Is The Seven Husbands of Evelyn Hugo a romance?

It contains a central sapphic love story but is literary fiction, not genre romance, so it doesn't guarantee a happy ending.
