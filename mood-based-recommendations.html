Romance Books by Mood: Pick a Feeling, Then a Book

Mood reading is the habit of choosing your next book based on how you feel and how you want to feel, rather than working through a fixed to-be-read pile. It's how most romance readers already behave, and it's why tools like StoryGraph's mood tags and EIKNA's mood search have caught on. A book that's perfect on a lazy Sunday can be wrong on a stressful Tuesday.

Below, each mood comes with what to expect, the tropes that tend to produce it, and books to start with.

Cozy and comforting

You want: low stakes, warm settings, kind characters, guaranteed happy ending. Tropes that deliver: small town, found family, cozy fantasy, holiday romance, bookshops and bakeries.

Can't Spell Treason Without Tea

Rebecca Thorne · 2023

sapphiccozy fantasyfound family

A queen's bodyguard and a powerful mage run away together to open a bookshop that serves tea. Gentle, funny and the book that put cozy sapphic fantasy on the map.

Delilah Green Doesn't Care

Ashley Herring Blake · 2022

sapphicsmall townwedding

Prickly heroine, soft love interest, a hometown full of history. Cozy with a little bite.

More: small-town lesbian romance, sapphic Christmas books and Eikna Romance's holiday romance hub.

Funny and light

You want: banter, comic set pieces, a fast pace. Tropes that deliver: fake dating, rivals, mistaken identity, opposites attract.

Written in the Stars

Alexandria Bellefleur · 2020

sapphicfake datingopposites attract

An astrologer and a sceptical actuary fake a relationship to get their families off their backs. Snappy and very quotable.

The Name Game

Beth O'Leary · 2026

mistaken identityisland

Two people called Charlie Jones are hired for the same island job. A comic premise with real feeling underneath.

More: sapphic fake dating books and opposites attract romance.

Swoony and yearning

You want: longing looks, almost-kisses, a payoff you've waited for. Tropes that deliver: slow burn, pining, friends to lovers, forbidden love.

Those Who Wait

Haley Cass · 2020

sapphicslow burnopposites

A long, patient slow burn between two women whose lives and ambitions pull in different directions. A favourite starting point for new sapphic readers.

One Last Stop

Casey McQuiston · 2021

sapphictime slipNew York

A cynical 23-year-old falls for a girl she meets on the subway, who turns out to be displaced in time from the 1970s.

More: our slow burn lesbian romance guide and Eikna Romance's slow burn hub.

Angsty and emotional

You want: to feel something, misunderstandings with real stakes, characters working through grief or fear. Tropes that deliver: second chance, hurt/comfort, illness, estranged families.

Here We Go Again

Alison Cochrun · 2024

sapphicenemies to loversroad trip

Two former best friends who now loathe each other take their dying former teacher on one last road trip. Funny and genuinely sad.

In Her Spotlight

Amy Spalding · 2026

sapphicsecond chanceHollywood

An actress reconnects with the director of her stage production, the ex she hurt years ago while she was still closeted.

More: sapphic second chance romance.

Dark and intense

You want: morally grey characters, danger, power imbalances, high stakes. Tropes that deliver: mafia, villain romance, captive, revenge, gothic. Always check content warnings first.

Matelda: In Silence We Forgive

Jossef S. · 2026

sapphicmafiasecrets

A dark sapphic mafia romance about a woman carrying secrets inside a crime family and the love that might survive them. The reading guide covers characters and content notes.

Dreamslayer

A.K. Mulford · December 1, 2026

sapphicdark romantasyenemies

An acolyte meant to ascend to the dream world falls for a nightmare and ends up in Hell, forced into an unholy alliance. The first book in a new dark trilogy.

Frequently asked questions

What is mood reading?

Mood reading means choosing your next book based on how you feel and how you want to feel, rather than following a fixed reading list.

What romance should I read if I want something cozy?

Try cozy fantasy like Can't Spell Treason Without Tea, small-town romance like Delilah Green Doesn't Care, or a holiday romance. These have low stakes and warm settings.

What romance books are good when I want to cry?

Second chance and road trip romances with grief threads work well, such as Here We Go Again by Alison Cochrun.

Can I search for books by mood online?

Yes. EIKNA lets you describe a mood in plain language and recommends from its catalogue, and Eikna Romance groups romance books by mood.
