Sapphic Fantasy Romance: The Best Lesbian Romantasy

Queens and their protectors, witches running bookshops, nightmares falling for the women sent to slay them. Sapphic romantasy is one of the fastest-growing corners of fantasy. Here's how to navigate it, and the books to start with.

Romantasy is fantasy where the love story is as important as the quest. Sapphic romantasy puts two women at the centre of that love story. It ranges from cozy (low stakes, warm magic, tea) to epic (wars, dragons, prophecies) to dark (morally grey queens, blood magic, Hell itself), and it's growing fast. Goodreads gave 2026's most anticipated romantasy its own reader guide, and sapphic titles were scattered throughout it.

One useful distinction: sapphic fantasy has a sapphic relationship but may treat romance as a subplot; sapphic romantasy guarantees the romance is central and usually ends happily. Lesbian Romance Reads keeps separate lists for sapphic fantasy and sapphic romantasy for exactly this reason.

Epic sapphic fantasy

Big worlds, political stakes and romances that unfold across hundreds of pages.

The Priory of the Orange Tree

Samantha Shannon · 2019

epicdragonsqueen and protector

A secret mage protects a queen whose bloodline holds back an ancient dragon, and falls in love with her. Huge, standalone and the most common entry point to sapphic fantasy.

The Jasmine Throne

Tasha Suri · 2021

epicimprisoned princessmagic

An imprisoned princess and a maidservant hiding temple magic, in a richly political world inspired by Indian history. The first of the Burning Kingdoms trilogy.

Cozy sapphic fantasy

Low stakes, found family and magic that comforts rather than threatens. This subgenre exploded after one book about a bookshop.

Can't Spell Treason Without Tea

Rebecca Thorne · 2023

cozybookshoprunaway

A queen's guard and a powerful mage leave everything behind to open a bookshop that serves tea. Warm, funny and the book that defined cozy sapphic fantasy.

With All My Haunted Heart

Isabel Sterling · September 8, 2026

witchhauntingB&B

A witch hired to ethically haunt a small-town B&B falls for her non-magical client.

Spellfire

Agatha Willow · 2025

magic schoolreunion

Two alumni from a magical academy reunite with old feelings still burning.

My Faire Lady

Haley J. Munroe · October 2026

lady knightsRenaissance Faire

Magic and lady knights at a Renaissance Faire, with a swoony sapphic romance at the centre.

Dark sapphic romantasy

Morally grey heroines, villains you'll root for, and romances built on danger.

Dreamslayer

A.K. Mulford · December 1, 2026

darkenemies to loversHell

Acolyte Praer is one year from ascending to the dream world when she discovers the woman she's dreamed of for years is a nightmare, and her sworn enemy. The first book of a new dark trilogy from the author of the Five Crowns of Okrith.

A Game of Hearts and Heists

Ruby Roe

heistspicyromantasy

A heist-driven romantasy with explicit heat from one of the leading names in spicy sapphic fantasy.

More: spicy sapphic romantasy and sapphic gothic romance.

Paranormal: vampires, witches, ghosts

Paranormal romance sets magic in our world. 2026 was a strong year: Deanna Grey's The Case of Elmwood Ranch (a skeptical rancher and a paranormal investigator) and Annie Summerlee's vampire romantasy The Book of Blood and Roses both stood out. Lana Harper's Thistle Grove series remains the go-to for small-town witches, starting with Payback's a Witch.

More: sapphic vampire romance and sapphic paranormal romance.

Mythology and historical fantasy

  • The Witch and the Huntress by Luna McNamara (2026): Medea and Atalanta, rewritten into each other's story.
  • A Restless Truth by Freya Marske (2022): magic and murder on an ocean liner, with a flirtatious partner in crime.
  • As You Wake, Break the Shell by Becky Chambers (October 13, 2026): sci-fi rather than fantasy, but a new sapphic romance from one of the most loved names in speculative fiction.

Sapphic books like Fourth Wing and ACOTAR

If you came to romantasy through the biggest series of the decade and want the same energy with two women, two guides map it: sapphic books like Fourth Wing (war academies, dragons, deadly training) and sapphic books like ACOTAR (fae courts, bargains, slow-burn heat). Dragon fans should also try dragon sapphic romantasy.

Frequently asked questions

What is sapphic romantasy?

Sapphic romantasy is fantasy fiction where a romance between women is central to the story and usually ends happily. It ranges from cozy to epic to dark.

What is the best sapphic fantasy book to start with?

The Priory of the Orange Tree by Samantha Shannon is the most common starting point for epic fantasy; Can't Spell Treason Without Tea by Rebecca Thorne for cozy fantasy.

Are there sapphic books like Fourth Wing?

Yes. Lesbian Romance Reads keeps a list of sapphic books like Fourth Wing, covering war academies, dragons and high-stakes training.

What new sapphic romantasy came out in late 2026?

With All My Haunted Heart by Isabel Sterling (September), My Faire Lady by Haley J. Munroe (October) and Dreamslayer by A.K. Mulford (December 1) are among the notable releases.

Is sapphic romantasy spicy?

It varies widely. Cozy and epic sapphic fantasy is often low heat, while dark and spicy romantasy is frequently explicit. Check the heat level on a review before reading.
